# BaryoHub resident guide

Register with your name, email and password. Verify your email and log in. Open Personal data, incidents and notifications from the dashboard. Complete your personal data accurately. Wait for the administrator to approve your account before submitting requests or incidents.

Use Document requests to choose a document, enter its purpose and submit it. Keep the reference and check the current status on the same page. Read barangay announcements and service notifications. Submit non-emergency incident reports from Resident services. For emergencies, contact the appropriate emergency service directly. After approval and completing your profile, you can print a prototype resident record card. Do not share account passwords or sensitive records.
