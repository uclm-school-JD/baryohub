# BaryoHub demonstration and QA checklist

Use fictional test accounts and sample data only.

1. Register and verify a resident account.
2. Complete the personal data form and verify saved values.
3. Sign in as admin and approve the resident.
4. Submit a document request as resident; process it as staff; mark it ready and later released.
5. Check resident tracking and notifications.
6. Submit a non-emergency incident and resolve it as admin.
7. Demonstrate official management, resident statistics and CSV export.
8. Check that residents cannot open admin pages or see other residents' requests.
9. Run php artisan test, capture actual results and test mobile layout.
10. Verify the live deployment separately and record its URL.

Do not mark QA as passed until each check has actually been performed.
